Studio Privacy Policy

At LensCraft Photography Studio, we treat your personal moments, contact records, and digital imagery with complete confidentiality, security, and artistic respect.

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER© 2026

This policy governs all digital services, inquiries, and booking sessions conducted via the LensCraft website (lenscraft.studio) and in-person at Studio 402, Highline Creative District, Lahore, Pakistan.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our studio or reserve a session, we collect information necessary to deliver exceptional visual services:

  • Contact Credentials: Full name, verified email address (anzaaleem1804@gmail.com coordination), and telephone or WhatsApp contact.
  • Session Specifications: Preferred calendar date, shoot venue / indoor location, selected photography discipline (Wedding, Fashion, Portrait, etc.), and artistic preferences.
  • Visual Data: Raw digital captures, high-resolution master files, and client-approved edits processed exclusively for your portfolio delivery.

2. How We Safeguard & Use Your Data

Your personal and session data is utilized strictly for service fulfillment:

  • Direct communication regarding booking confirmations, prep-call styling, and timeline coordination.
  • Provision of private, password-protected online client galleries with secure cloud download links.
  • We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal details or shoot photographs to third-party advertisers.

3. Client Image Rights & Social Showcase

Every client retains personal printing and distribution rights for delivered master galleries. LensCraft respects your privacy: if you request an entirely private wedding or discreet VIP commercial session without online portfolio publication, we formally execute a complete Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) with zero public posting.

4. Digital Gallery Retention

Digital master galleries are hosted on encrypted cloud storage for a guaranteed minimum period of 12 months from delivery date. Clients are encouraged to download and create offline backups upon final gallery delivery.
