OPERATIONAL STANDARDS & ATELIER CHARTER

By reserving a session online or signing a photography commission contract with LensCraft Photography Studio, you agree to the following terms and operational standards.

1. Session Reservations & Retainers

To formally block studio equipment, crew schedules, and calendar dates:

  • A 30% non-refundable reservation retainer is required upon formal booking confirmation.
  • The remaining 70% balance is payable prior to shoot commencement or on the day of production.
  • Invoices are issued in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) for national bookings or equivalent USD / EUR for international assignments.

2. Rescheduling & Weather Contingency

We accommodate unforeseen life events with flexibility. Clients may reschedule a scheduled session without penalty up to 72 hours prior to call time. For outdoor weddings and natural landscape shoots impacted by severe rain or force majeure weather, sessions pivot seamlessly to our climate-controlled studio or an agreed alternative date.

3. Production Timeline & Delivery Guarantees

Every photograph undergoes meticulous bespoke color-grading and high-fidelity retouching:

  • Sneak Peek Highlights: Delivered via private online gallery within 48 to 72 hours of session wrap.
  • Complete Master Gallery: Basic Package (7 business days), Standard Package (4 business days), Premium Package (48 hours express).
  • Handcrafted Keepsake Albums: Physical Italian leather albums require 3 to 4 weeks for artisanal bookbinding and direct doorstep delivery.

4. Copyright & Usage Licenses

Clients receive an unlimited personal reproduction license permitting lifetime printing, framing, and personal digital sharing. Commercial advertising rights and third-party vendor sublicensing are included in our commercial and corporate packages.
